EWS Score Analyzer for Patients with Diabetes

This algorithm processes patient-level EWS data to extract each patient's highest risk score during hospitalization and plots its distribution by outcome. Given a user-defined threshold, it computes sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, and accuracy with 95% confidence intervals, with optional subgroup analysis.

Knowledge

EWS Diagnostic Accuracy Analysis for Patients with Diabetes

ID: EWSScoreAnalysis

Type: Knowledge

Description: An algorithm that processes patient-level Early Warning System (EWS) score data — one row per patient, with multiple time-point score columns and a binary mortality outcome — to compute each patient's highest risk score, plot its distribution by outcome, and evaluate diagnostic accuracy (sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, accuracy with 95% Wilson confidence intervals) at a user-defined threshold, with optional subgroup analysis (e.g. medical vs. surgical patients).

Documentation

  • Performance of an Algorithm Predicting Mortality in Medical vs Surgical Patients with Diabetes

    Published conference abstract (ECEESPE2025) presenting the clinical study that motivated this knowledge object. Reports EWS model performance in 15,727 hospitalized diabetic patients: medical group (n=10,234) accuracy 85%, sensitivity 89.78%, specificity 85.11%; surgical group (n=5,493) accuracy 81.83%, sensitivity 66.67%, specificity 81.85%. NPV exceeds 99% in both groups.
